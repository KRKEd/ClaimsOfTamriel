I  ·  The World

An Ontology of the Contested

Why Skyrim?

The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim is an unusually rich KR domain because it deliberately withholds ground truth. The same territory, deity, or political act is interpreted by different factions in ways that cannot be reconciled — not due to incomplete data, but because the contradictions are constitutive of the world.

Unlike a typical ontology that encodes facts, this project encodes claims: assertions held by specific agents, grounded in specific evidence, and in explicit conflict with other assertions.

The same event — the death of a High King, the divinity of Talos, the legitimacy of a resistance movement — is simultaneously true and false depending on who is speaking.

The Design Principle

Epistemic pluralism is operationalized as a structural commitment: no claim is marked as correct, no faction as authoritative. Every assertion is relativized to its holder. The ontology models the structure of disagreement, not its resolution.

This architecture is domain-neutral: the same pattern — Claim, Holder, ClaimType, three conflict relations, contested evidence — applies equally to news media bias, historical revisionism, or legal testimony.

USE CASES

Real Case Uses for This Project

This project is not just a stylized game ontology; it is a proof-of-concept for structurally representing disagreement, evidence, and claim provenance in domains where truth is contested.

Here are four grounded applications that show how a claim-centered ontology can turn conflict into machine-readable, objective structure.

  • 1

    Objective conflict resolution in real-world ontologies

    This approach enables ontologies to capture real-world disputes without choosing sides, letting both factual and artificial information emerge from structured comparison of competing claims.

  • 2

    Analyzing fictional worlds across sociology and beyond

    Fictional universes become test beds for sociological, cultural, and epistemic modelling, where conflicting narratives can be represented, compared, and studied with the same machinery used for real disputes.

  • 3

    Journalistic Fact-Check Frameworks

    A machine-readable model for comparing contradictory news sources claim by claim, showing which assertion depends on which evidence and making contested reporting structurally transparent.

  • 4

    Legal & Testimonial Evidence Mapping

    In court-like workflows, this ontology can represent contradictory witness statements, who claimed what, and on what evidence those claims rest — preserving the structure of legal dispute.

II  ·  The Conflict

Three Laws of Disagreement

Most ontologies encode the world as it is. This ontology encodes the world as it is argued to be. The challenge is not to represent facts but to represent the social and epistemic structure of factual dispute.

Three theoretical commitments shape the architecture. Claims are first-class reified entities. Conflict is typed — not all disagreements carry the same logical weight. Neutrality is representable as structured absence.

The ontology does not take sides. It models the structure of taking sides — who claims what, against whom, on what grounds, and through what kind of opposition.

  • I

    Logical Contradiction

    contradicts — Symmetric (owl:SymmetricProperty). Strict logical opposition: if one is true, the other must be false. Used for 6 of 8 pairs — sovereignty, theology, territorial claims.

  • II

    Interpretive Difference

    disputes — Symmetric. Conflicting interpretations of the same entity where both positions could be valid within their own framework. Used for 2 pairs: the cosmological and moral-philosophical disputes.

  • III

    Active Rejection

    denies — Directional, asymmetric. Active, explicit repudiation. Only the Talos pair uses it in both directions — making it the most adversarial dispute. Not just disagreement: propaganda.

VI  ·  Discoveries

What the Ontology Reveals

Non-obvious findings extractable through SPARQL — knowledge not stated anywhere in the ontology but emergent from its structure.

◆

The Dragonborn as Cosmic Arbiter

Frames claims only in existential pairs (moral, cosmological, vampiric). Never territorial or political. This asymmetry — queryable via ClaimType filter — positions them as an agent who intervenes at the level of fate, not governance.

♦

Contested Evidence

Auriel's Bow and the Elder Scroll are each cited by directly opposing claims. The same artifact simultaneously grounds a liberation argument and a prevention argument. Contested evidence, not contested fact.

∅

Machine-Readable Neutrality

Balgruuf's political neutrality is encoded as the absence of framesAs assertions. A single FILTER NOT EXISTS query returns exactly one result: him. Structured absence is a valid KR technique.

→

Denial is Rare & Propagandistic

Only the Talos pair uses the directional denies relation — and both sides use it. This bidirectionality marks it as uniquely adversarial: not just logical contradiction but active propaganda. Queryable and provable.
